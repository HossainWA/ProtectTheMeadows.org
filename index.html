Before you take the photo

Your selfie is evidence.

A photo taken in the flowers proves the flowers are gone. Take it from the trail and it looks even better.

Great shots from the trail

  • Crouch low on the path so the flowers fill the front of your photo.
  • Zoom in instead of stepping in. 2x or 3x does the walking for you.
  • Put the mountain behind you and ask a friend to shoot from the trail.
  • Go early or near sunset, when the light is soft.

You can help in a minute

Walk the trailIt's built for your feet. The flowers stay for the next visitor.
Picnic on the pathBlankets, chairs and lunch belong on the trail or in a picnic area, not in the flowers.
Park in marked spotsBumpers and tires crush meadow plants too.
Tell a friendOne kind reminder keeps a whole meadow standing.

Be a meadow keeper

Every person who stays on the trail gives bees, butterflies and birds a meadow to come back to. Thank you.

It's also the rule. At Mount Rainier, leaving the trail in several meadow areas can bring a fine of up to $5,000. Rules differ by park. 2025 Superintendent's Compendium
